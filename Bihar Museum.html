Aboute the Museum

The Bihar Museum, located in Patna, is the one of India's most morden and beautiful museum.
It was inaugurated in 2015 and is designed to showcase the rich History, art, and culture of Bihar.

What's make It Special

The museum is divided into serval galleries-including the Children' gallery, Oritentating artifacts from ancient to morden time.
The buildings architecture is a blend of traditional and morden design, fering a calm and inspiring atomsphere.
